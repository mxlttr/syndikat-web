Last Sunday, 6 October, the flying-plastic enthusiasts from [Disc Golf Niederrhein invited players for the tenth time](https://turniere.discgolf.de/index.php&p=events&sp=view&id=2222) to a two-round disc flying show on the picturesque grounds of the Auesee lake.

The area around the Auesee, which also includes a lido, was only recently expanded with new disc golf holes. Now players can enjoy their beloved throwing sport on 15 holes outside of tournaments, too. But watch out! Miss the target and your disc might end up in the water. So bring your swimming trunks.

Despite glorious sunshine on this Sunday in October, several Syndikat players attended the tournament without swimming trunks. (After all, real pros from Cologne let others fish the discs out of the water.)

After the first round, Benedikt Pöpsel led his clubmates with a score of –3, putting him in a shared 4th place. Without having played the course beforehand – or "playing blind", as the jargon goes – he managed a top result on demanding holes.

![Participants in the Ring of Fire at the Wesel Open](/assets/images/posts/wesel-ring-of-fire.jpg)

During the lunch break came one of the tournament's highlights: the call for the so-called "Ring of Fire" – a putting competition in which all participants stand around the same basket at a distance of 10 metres and throw their discs at the basket simultaneously on a signal. If you hit, you stay. If you miss, you're out. After four rounds, Syndikat player Alexander Kraft emerged victorious from the contest. He even managed to triumph over the reigning German putting champion, Severin Hövel of Bonnsai e.V.

![Alexander Kraft wins the Ring of Fire at the Wesel Open](/assets/images/posts/wesel-ring-of-fire-gewinner.jpg)

Inspired by this success, the field went into the second round of the tournament, where players had to battle increasingly strong winds. Whether because of the wind or other factors (as everyone knows, in disc golf it's never your own fault), Benedikt Pöpsel couldn't repeat his result and, with a +3 in the second round, finished in 11th place. Alexander Kraft, on the other hand, whose surname means "strength" and who clearly hadn't run out of it yet, matched his first-round result in round 2 and finished 7th with a total of –4 over two rounds.

Congratulations to the winners, and a big thank you to Disc Golf Niederrhein e.V. for the great organisation and a thoroughly successful event.
