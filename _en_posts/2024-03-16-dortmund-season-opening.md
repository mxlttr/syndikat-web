On Saturday, 16 March, we were at the *Drive Season Opening* tournament in Dortmund, organised by [Drive Discgolf](https://www.drivediscgolf.de).

### Getting there
To make it to Dortmund on time, we had to set off from Cologne towards Revierpark Wischlingen at 6:00 am.
This time everyone made it to Dortmund without trouble, so Guido, Patrick, Jonas, Alex K. and Philipp were ready at the tee on time.

### Rain from above and mud from below
Unfortunately, it had rained the night before and practically until a few minutes before the start, so the first round in particular turned into a bit of a mud fight.

![Mud](/assets/images/posts/24_driveopen_schlamm.jpg)

After a few hours, though, the sun came out, and once again you could feel that the start of spring was not far away.

### A happy finish

After the first round, Drive Discgolf provided a little refreshment and we were able to browse the well-stocked disc shop. Shopping was all the easier because the player pack included a voucher from [Drive Discgolf](https://www.drivediscgolf.de).

![Balancing across the lake](/assets/images/posts/24_drive_see.jpg)

The second round followed soon after, and after 18 more holes the tournament was already over.
You can see the results on [the PDGA website](https://www.pdga.com/tour/event/78800).
In the end, two of our [friends from the Bonnsais](https://www.bonnsai.org/hardtwerfen-ii/) made it to the top places in their divisions.
Congratulations to Severin (T3 in MA1) and Fozzy (T2 in MA4). Fozzy then went on to win the playoff.
Speaking of the Bonnsais: [Hardtwerfen II](https://www.bonnsai.org/hardtwerfen-ii/) takes place on 28 April, and you can still sign up.
