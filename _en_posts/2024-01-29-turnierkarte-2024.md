The 2024 disc golf season is about to begin, and the anticipation among disc golfers is almost tangible. But while everyone is eagerly waiting to let their discs fly again, we have prepared something special to make this season even more memorable – our brand-new [disc golf tournament map for Germany](/en/turniere/)!

The idea came from a gap on the official website, which lacked a clear map of upcoming tournaments. So we took the initiative ourselves and created this interactive map, which will guide the disc golf community through the entire tournament season.

### Everything at a glance: an overview of the tournament landscape

Tired of laboriously scrolling through lists looking for the next disc golf events? Our tournament map brings everything together in one place. Whether you are an experienced player or just getting into the world of disc golf – with just one click, you get a comprehensive overview of all current tournaments in Germany.

### Data source: turniere.discgolf.de

The information on our map comes directly from [turniere.discgolf.de](https://turniere.discgolf.de), the database for official disc golf tournaments in Germany. We have focused on presenting this data clearly and in a user-friendly way to make sure you are always up to date.
The disc golf community deserves a central place for all tournament information, and that is exactly what our map offers. We understand the passion and dedication that come with the sport of disc golf and wanted to create a resource that reflects this enthusiasm.

### Discover the map now!

[Visit our disc golf tournament map](/en/turniere/) and be well prepared for the 2024 season. Whether you are looking for new challenges, just searching for tournaments nearby or simply want to stay up to date – our map makes it easier than ever.

The 2024 season is going to be great – let's conquer the baskets together and let the hyzer flips fly!
