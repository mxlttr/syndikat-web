Disc Golf Syndikat from Cologne is known for its passion for this exciting sport. Sure, we love playing disc golf, but we love it even more when we do it together on new courses. This time, our sense of adventure took us to Arnhem, where 16 of us had a lovely day out. In this blog post, we take you along on our unforgettable trip, which was all about sport, camaraderie and exploration.

### Setting off for Arnhem

Our journey began in Cologne, where we met early in the morning and set off for Arnhem with fully packed bags and fresh (some of them floating) discs. The anticipation was almost tangible as we got ready for the day on the train and in the cars. The atmosphere was electric, because everyone knew an exciting course was waiting for us.

![Alex putting](/assets/images/posts/alex.jpg)

### Water from above and below

After arriving in Arnhem, we headed straight to the disc golf course. This was the centrepiece of our trip, and we were ready to show what we could do. We formed flights and got an overview of the 18 holes in the first round.

Our members had been looking forward to this event for a long time, and it was great to see everyone giving their best. It rained, frogs hopped through the wet grass and discs flew through the air. Amid this wonderful setting, it became clear that disc golf is not just about competition, but also about community and the fun of the game.

![Pete celebrating his putt](/assets/images/posts/pete.jpg)

The [course in Immerloo Park](https://udisc.com/courses/immerloo-park-6pMm) stands out for its tricky water hazards. Sadly, we have to mourn the loss of a few loyal discs, but replacements will surely be found.

### A tasty lunch break

After the exciting first round, there was another must-do on our list: Arnhem is known not only for disc golf, but also for its history, culture and delicious frikandel speciaal. We took the opportunity to try the local delicacy at a nearby chip shop. Freshly fortified and with dried clothes, we then set off for round 2!

### Camaraderie and togetherness

![Philipp putting out of the bushes](/assets/images/posts/philipp.jpg)

One of the highlights of our club trip was strengthening our community. During the rounds, we had the chance to get to know each other better and make new friends. Whether beginner or experienced player, everyone was encouraged and supported. That's what makes our club so special – the camaraderie and togetherness we share.

### Conclusion: an unforgettable adventure

Our club trip to Arnhem was a complete success. Not only did we see exciting drives and putts, we finally got a taste of international disc golf, too. We will remember this trip for a long time, and we are already looking forward to the next adventures our club will take on.

Disc golf not only connects people through sport, it also creates opportunities for unforgettable experiences and adventures. If you are not yet part of a club, [you should definitely consider joining one](/en/register/). You will not only improve your skills but also make new friends and explore the world – all in the name of flying discs!

Are you ready to start your own disc golf adventure? Then grab a disc and join in – a world full of fun and possibilities is waiting for you!
