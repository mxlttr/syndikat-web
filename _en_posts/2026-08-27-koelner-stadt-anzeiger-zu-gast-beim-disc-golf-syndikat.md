The [Kölner Stadt-Anzeiger visited Disc Golf Syndikat in Weidenpesch](https://www.ksta.de/koeln/nippes/weidenpesch/discgolf-syndikat-weidenpesch-scheiben-werfen-ganz-ohne-vorkenntnisse-1356384) (article in German). For us, this is a great opportunity to show more people in Cologne what happens on our course at Scheibenstraße: throwing together, learning from each other and having a good time.

## Just give disc golf a try

At first glance, disc golf can look like a sport full of special discs and complicated throws. But that shouldn't put anyone off for their first visit. No prior knowledge is needed, and we have discs on site for you to try.

At training, people with different levels of experience come together. Some work on their putts, others want to learn their first controlled throw or simply play a round in a group. It's exactly this mix that makes getting started easy.

## Syndication Weekly on Tuesday evenings

The Tuesday evening mentioned in the article is part of our weekly league, the **Syndication Weekly**. In summer, we meet on Tuesdays at 6:30 pm at the [course at Scheibenstraße](https://goo.gl/maps/yqyVDEoEs8Qd5LD56). If you are new to Cologne, want to try disc golf or are looking to connect with other players, feel free to come along.

On our page [Disc Golf in Cologne](/en/disc-golf-koeln/), you will find an overview of the course, league, training and club. If you want to play straight away, you can also find the latest information on our [events page](/en/events/).

## Come to the next Syndication Weekly

You don't need your own equipment for your first visit. You can find the latest information about the next round on our [events page](/en/events/). If you would rather work on your technique in a focused way, you can also join our [club training](/en/training/). The [membership page](/en/register/) explains how you can become part of the Syndikat.
