In the long run, the course at Scheibenstraße gets a bit monotonous. On YouTube and social media, you inevitably come across the disc golf dice game, which is meant to help with exactly that.

We built a digital version that runs in your smartphone browser. [You can try it out here](/en/disc-dice/).

The dice decide which disc-and-throw combination you have to throw from the tee. Hyzer putter? Anhyzer driver? Lots of unusual combinations are possible and force you to try out new throws.

By the way: with the joker, you get to choose any disc or throw.
