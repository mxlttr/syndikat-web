Spring is just around the corner, and with it a new season full of events for Disc Golf Syndikat from Cologne. The days will soon be getting longer again and the temperatures milder, which means it's time to get outside and enjoy the game to the fullest.

Disc golf is a fast-growing sport that is finding more and more fans in Cologne. It is a combination of golf and frisbee in which players try to throw a disc into a basket, similar to a game of golf. The Disc Golf Syndikat club gives everyone who is interested the opportunity to play this sport and take part in competitions.

During the coming season, we will continue to [host our weekly league](https://udisc.com/leagues/syndication-weekly), where players of every level are welcome. Whether you are an experienced player or just want to try the game, you are warmly invited to join us.

We will also offer regular training sessions during the season, where we can improve our skills and learn from experienced players. It is a great way to meet new people and become part of a growing community.

This year we want to focus in particular on setting clear training goals to help every player improve their skills and achieve their goals. We try to work closely with each player to develop individual training plans tailored to their strengths and weaknesses. We believe that with clear goals and targeted training, every player can reach their full potential and keep improving. So what are you waiting for? Join us for a training session and start your journey to becoming a better disc golf player in 2023!

We are really looking forward to seeing you at our events and training sessions this season!
