On 24 February, Disc Golf Syndikat headed to Rüsselsheim for a few rounds of disc golf. We were drawn by the [varied and challenging course in Ostpark](https://udisc.com/courses/ruesselsheim-ostpark-wYvu).

### Getting to Rüsselsheim

The journey went by faster than expected.
After settling once and for all which potatoes are best for potato salad, we had already arrived.
Although the drive was dampened by a few rain showers, the weather seemed to be on our side: the clouds parted and the sun graced us with its presence.
Here, too, you could feel that spring was slowly on its way.

So by 10 am we were ready at the course and could start our first round.
A few players from the local club [Scheibensucher e.V.](http://www.scheibensucher.de/wp/) also welcomed us.

### Throw by throw

![Kerstin on the tee](/assets/images/posts/rue_wurf.jpeg)

With its 24 varied holes, the disc golf course in Rüsselsheim proved to be a real challenge. From open meadows to dense woods, every section offered a new and exciting playing situation.
Even if the line wasn't always clear, we made our way from basket to basket with tips for each other and the occasional trick.

![Agreeing on the best line isn't always easy](/assets/images/posts/rue_hands_up.jpeg)


There was always a healthy dose of luck involved, too.

![Basket reached... no matter how!](/assets/images/posts/rue_close.jpg)

Despite some challenging holes, we didn't let ourselves be put off and managed to play two full rounds.

![Moritz gives it everything](/assets/images/posts/rue_full_spin.jpeg)

When the last holes had been played and the sun was slowly disappearing below the horizon, we had a small snack to recharge and headed back to Cologne, exhausted but happy.

![Heading back to Cologne](/assets/images/posts/rue_abfahrt.jpeg)

All in all, it was a great day for discovering a new disc golf course and simply having some fun on and off the course. If you would also like to join future trips, just keep an eye out <a href="#" onclick="window.open('\/\/community.syndikat.golf');">on our Discord</a>.
