2022 is behind us, and with it the first 4 months of our weekly league "Syndication Weekly". Since August, 197 rounds have been played by 44 players. Across 20 dates, up to 21 disc golfers came together at a time, throwing the disc more than 5,000 times in total at the course in Cologne.

You can find the results so far and more [statistics on UDisc](https://udisc.com/leagues/syndication-weekly/1-Jan-ECG8?tab=standings).

The event has now become well established, and of course the series will continue in the new year. During the cold season we will stick with Sundays (1 pm); once it stays light for longer, we will switch back to Tuesday evenings.

Disc golf beginners are still very welcome to gain their first "tournament experience" in a relaxed atmosphere.

We look forward to your feedback. How did you like the first events, and what can be improved? Feel free to send an [email to birdie@syndikat.golf](mailto:birdie@syndikat.golf)
