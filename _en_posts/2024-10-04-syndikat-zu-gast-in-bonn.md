Last Sunday, 29 September 2024, Bonnsai e.V. invited players to the [first Bonnsai Open](https://turniere.discgolf.de/index.php?p=events&sp=view&id=2172) in the [Derletal at Hardtberg](https://udisc.com/courses/discgolf-derletal-fz1M). For 72 players in seven divisions, plus numerous helpers and spectators, the weather was at its best, so nothing stood in the way of a successful tournament.

In addition to the nine permanent holes in the Derletal, the Bonnsais had installed nine extra baskets especially for the event to create a varied and challenging course. The layout led from open fields and meadows with steep slopes, through narrow fairways in the woods, to the technical holes on the grounds of the Hardtberg swimming pool, demanding the full range of throws from the players.

For the Syndikat's 11 participants, things got serious at 9:05 am sharp. It quickly became clear that the competition would be a real challenge that day. After the first 18 holes, Alexander Kraft was our best performer with a score of –4 over 18 holes and a shared 4th place, giving him a good starting position for the second round. There, however, the top 3 showed their full abilities, so in the end he narrowly missed out on the podium.

![Bonnsai Open award ceremony with the podium](/assets/images/posts/podium-bonnsai-open.jpg)

With an outstanding score of –11, Severin Hövel secured first place, and his precision amazed onlookers time and again. The same goes for Klaus Kattwinkel, who won the Masters 50 division with a total of –16 over two rounds and played the two best rounds of the tournament overall. The highlight of the day came from Claus Erkenrath, who went down in history in the Masters 40 division with an ace.

Once again, you can only take your hat off to the organisation of the entire tournament. The extra holes blended seamlessly into the classic layout and effortlessly won the players over. Everything ran completely smoothly and without fuss, which is anything but a given at tournaments of this size.

The success of the 1st Bonnsai Open shows once again that disc golf is increasingly popular in the Rhineland and is establishing itself more broadly. One thing that makes the sport unique is, and remains, its community, made up of a wide range of committed people from all kinds of backgrounds. Despite the competition, this creates a friendly atmosphere that is always visible in conversations on and off the course, and ensures that the main thing about the hobby is never lost – the joy of the sport.

We at Disc Golf Syndikat would like to thank our neighbours for a fantastic tournament, the helpers for their support and the City of Bonn, which provided the land for the extra holes and thus made the event possible in the first place. We very much hope that the Bonnsai Open will establish itself as a home-grown name in the future and that we can celebrate our sport in the Derletal for many years to come.
