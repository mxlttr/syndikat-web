Spring is here, and we are kicking off the new season with a special event! On 30 March 2025, the Syndikat is heading off on a disc golf trip to Bad Fredeburg. A varied course in beautiful natural surroundings awaits us there – perfect for all skill levels.

Whether you are experienced or completely new to the sport – everyone is welcome! Pack your discs and join us for a day of sport and good company.

👉 Sign-up: If you would like to join, simply sign up on our <a href="#" onclick="window.open('\/\/community.syndikat.golf');">Syndikat Discord</a>! Questions can be answered there.

Stay tuned and save the date! 🚀
