On Saturday, 10 August, we were represented by a total of six players at the [Westfalentour in Bielefeld](https://frisbee-nrw.de/2024/07/21/wt-08-2024-am-obersee-in-bielefeld/).
The tournament took place on the course at [Obersee](https://udisc.com/courses/bielefeld-disc-golf-obersee-YdTG).

## Tournament rounds and results

Thanks to the many helpers and [TuS Ost Bielefeld](https://www.tus-ost.de/discgolf) for preparing the course and for the great organisation.
Since the Westfalentour is always played on a 12-hole course, the regular layout was changed slightly.
Even though I personally struggled with the woods holes and hit plenty of trees, my fellow competitors fared better.

After the usual two WT rounds in great weather, Max and Raphael came out on top in their divisions.
Max was delighted with third place in the A division and Raphael with first place in the C division.
Radek Scharte ([YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@radekscharte)) was there too and grabbed 3rd place in the C division.

![Congratulations](/assets/images/posts/24_wt8_winners.jpg)

During the break afterwards, we enjoyed delicious soups and cakes, which the organisers provided free of charge.
This was followed by the NRW Cup round.
You can [view the results on Metrix via this link](https://discgolfmetrix.com/2961980).
