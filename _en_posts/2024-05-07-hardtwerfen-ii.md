Last weekend, on Sunday, 28 April 2024 to be precise, the disc golf tournament "Hardtwerfen II" was held in the idyllic Derletal in Bonn. For this special event, the organisers from the [Bonnsai club](https://bonnsai.org) had created 9 additional holes alongside the 9 permanent ones, winding creatively through the adjacent outdoor pool and the surrounding landscape. This gave the tournament a unique character and presented the participants with exciting new challenges.

![Severin putting at Hardtwerfen II in Bonn](/assets/images/posts/hardtwerfen-severin.jpg)

Disc Golf Syndikat, represented by 11 players, experienced a day full of sporting success and great conversations within the community. The participants showed great commitment and impressive performances on the extended 18-hole course.

In the MA1 division, Severin from Bonn showed his skill and confidently secured first place. His technique and precision on the demanding holes were impressive, and he ultimately finished two throws ahead of his pursuers, who included the German champion in the Masters 50 division. In the MA2 division, our player Alex impressed with a strong and consistent performance that earned him a well-deserved win. His ability to stay calm under pressure and throw precisely was the key to his success.

> You can find all results here: [Hardtwerfen II on discgolf.de](https://turniere.discgolf.de/index.php?p=events-livescoring&p=events&sp=live&id=2171)

In the MA4 division, no fewer than 3 club members were in the lead flight, but they couldn't defend that lead in the second round. In fact, it was Raphael, another member who had been 5 throws behind after the first round, who made it into the playoff for the win with a remarkable performance and was ultimately rewarded with second place.

There were exciting moments in the women's divisions too: Kerstin from our ranks competed in the FA4 division and showed great commitment. Unfortunately, she also narrowly lost in a playoff and took second place.

![Members of Disc Golf Syndikat at Hardtwerfen II in Bonn](/assets/images/posts/hardtwerfen-team.jpg)

The course itself was a real challenge, especially the holes designed around the outdoor pool. Players had to manoeuvre their discs along some very narrow fairways and stay away from OB.

The Bonnsai club deserves special recognition for the excellent organisation of the tournament. The seamless integration of the additional holes and the overall logistics were exemplary, which all participants greatly appreciated.

All in all, "Hardtwerfen II" was a complete success and proof of how much the sport of disc golf is growing in the region and how lively the community is. We at Disc Golf Syndikat are proud of our players and grateful for the opportunity to take part in such a well-organised event. We are already looking forward to the next edition of the tournament and will once again be there in large numbers.
