Dear members and friends of the Syndikat,

we are delighted to warmly invite you to the Cologne Disc Golf Summer 2023! This year we finally want to bring together all of Cologne's disc golfers to spend a fun afternoon together. The date is **22 July**, starting **at 2 pm**.

<a href="/assets/misc/cdgs23.ics" class="button button--primary">Add to calendar</a>

The summer party will of course take place at the disc golf course at Scheibenstraße, and we are preparing a wide range of activities. Naturally, you can play a round or two of disc golf, but there will also be lots of games.

We have planned some exciting competitions! Take part in the "Ring of Fire", the putting challenge where the most consistent thrower wins. Show off your precise putting in the putting challenge. In the CTP challenge (closest to the pin), you can test your accuracy and prove your approach skills.

For all disc golfers, we have also organised a **disc swap**. Bring your unused discs and swap them with other players. That way you can expand your collection and maybe find the perfect disc for your game.

Of course, a summer party wouldn't be complete without other games. There will be a slackline, kubb, DDC and more. Come along, **bring your family and friends** and spend an unforgettable day with other disc golf enthusiasts.

After sunset, there will also be a glow round, for which the baskets are lit up and we play with glowing discs.

Food and drink are taken care of! We offer a selection of drinks at low prices so you can refresh yourselves during the event.

<a href="/assets/misc/cdgs23.ics">Mark **22 July at 2 pm** in your calendar</a> and be part of this great event! We look forward to seeing you there and to an unforgettable afternoon full of disc golf, competitions and community.
