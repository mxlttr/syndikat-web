> **Update:** Since 30 October, we have been meeting at the disc golf course every Sunday! Tee-off at 1 pm.

Despite the dismantling work at Scheibenstraße, there will be [a small summer league](https://udisc.com/leagues/syndication-weekly) starting in August. Players of all levels are invited to take part and collect points over the coming weeks.

Under the name "Syndication Weekly", we meet **on Tuesdays at 7:30 pm** at the course at Scheibenstraße. Things kick off on 2 August!

We will play a 9-hole round (old layout plus tee pad 2 to basket 4 and tee pad 5 to basket 1).

![League layout](/assets/images/posts/scheibenstrasse_layout.jpg)

Registration and scoring run via the [free UDisc app](https://udisc.com/). Registration is already open, so sign up quickly!

The tournament/league director is Philipp; questions and suggestions can be sent to [birdie@syndikat.golf](mailto:birdie@syndikat.golf).

[Go straight to the league on UDisc.](https://udisc.com/leagues/syndication-weekly)
