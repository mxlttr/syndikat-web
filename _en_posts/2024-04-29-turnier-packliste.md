Is your next tournament coming up and you are not sure whether you have packed everything you need? Of course, the basics like discs, a marker and your smartphone go without saying. But we want to share a few insider tips with you that can make your tournament experience not only more pleasant but perhaps even more successful. Here they are, the little helpers that are often overlooked!

{% include snippets/checklist.html %}

## 1. Towels – yes, several!
You should pack not just one but several towels. Why? Because you never know what the weather will be like! One towel for wet discs, one for sweat and maybe an extra one in case you get caught in a shower. Dry discs mean better throws! You can also use disinfectant spray to clean sticky hands after snacking.

## 2. Umbrella and rain jacket
No matter how good the weather forecast looks, a rain jacket is a must. Light, compact models that don't take up much space in your bag are particularly practical. If rain is forecast, an umbrella definitely makes sense. Golf shops sell large models that keep you and your bag/cart dry.

## 3. Snacks and water
This may sound obvious, but the type of snacks can make a big difference. Choose something that gives you energy without weighing you down. Nuts, energy bars or fruit such as apples and bananas are great. And when it comes to water – better one bottle too many than one too few, especially on hot days! Make sure not to eat sticky snacks so you keep your hands clean.

## 4. A pair of spare socks
Yes, you read that right! A fresh, dry pair of socks can be a real blessing, especially if your tournament lasts all day. Wet or sandy feet are really unpleasant and can disrupt your concentration.

## 5. Small first aid kit
A few plasters, some disinfectant spray and perhaps a pain-relieving ointment never hurt. In the heat of the moment, a mishap can happen quickly, and it's better to be prepared.

## 6. Pen and paper
A small waterproof notepad and a pen that writes even when wet are really helpful. You can make notes about your throws or about the holes, which can help you learn and improve later.

## 7. Backup discs
Sure, you have your favourite discs with you, but what if one gets lost? Packing a few backup discs for emergencies can save you a lot of stress. Especially on unfamiliar courses, where it's easy to lose a disc in the bushes.

## 8. Sunscreen and insect repellent
Often forgotten and yet so important. Sunburn or constant crawling and stinging insects can really ruin your day and your performance. Apply sunscreen with the hand you don't throw with to avoid slippery hands.

## 9. Sandpaper for disc care
An often overlooked trick among disc golfers is carrying sandpaper. Small nicks and rough spots can appear on your discs during play and affect how the disc feels in your hand. With a bit of sandpaper, you can quickly smooth out these irregularities and get your discs back in top shape. A small piece is quite enough and takes up hardly any space in your bag.

## 10. Sunglasses – not just a style factor
A good pair of sunglasses is more than just a fashion accessory – they are essential for protecting your eyes on long, sunny tournament days. Glare from the sun can affect the accuracy of your throws and tire your eyes over the course of the day. Sunglasses with UV protection ensure that you can see the holes clearly and concentrate better on your game. They also help reduce eye strain caused by constantly blinking and squinting against the bright sun.

## 11. Power bank – always stay connected
A long tournament day can be exhausting for your smartphone too, especially if you use apps for scorekeeping or GPS functions for course navigation. Having a power bank in your bag means you never risk losing track of your scores or becoming unreachable because of a dead battery. It can also be very handy if you want to listen to music during a break or show your fellow players the latest memes. With a power bank, you are always ready, no matter how long the day gets.

## Conclusion

With these tips, you are not only well equipped but also prepared for pretty much any situation that might come your way during a disc golf tournament. So pack your bag, grab your discs and head off to the next tournament! Good luck and, more importantly: have fun!
