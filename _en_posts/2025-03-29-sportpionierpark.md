Cologne is sending a strong signal for the sport of disc golf! With the new putting area in Sportpionierpark, the city gains a great new training opportunity for beginners and pros alike. The four baskets, each combined with three throwing positions and distances of up to 22 metres, offer plenty of variety. Trees as natural obstacles and a clever wooden element that you have to throw through precisely make the area a real challenge for players of all skill levels.

![Entrance sign to the disc golf putting area](/assets/images/posts/sportpionierpark_schild.jpg)

As Disc Golf Syndikat, we are thrilled about this development! The new area will not only enrich our regular putting training, but also offers an ideal basis for a putting league and relaxed evening rounds. A big thank you to the City of Cologne, which created this great opportunity as part of the development of Parkstadt Süd.

## A park with a future: the role of the Pionierpark

The Pionierpark is a key project for Cologne: work has been under way here since June 2021 to make the area usable for the city's residents. The highlight? The completion of the Inner Green Belt! By unsealing former industrial land, the green corridor from Eifelwall to the banks of the Rhine is being closed – a historic opportunity for Cologne. The new open space not only connects the surrounding neighbourhoods but also creates valuable space for recreation, sport and social life.

![A disc golf putting hole with an obstacle](/assets/images/posts/sportpionierpark_hindernis.jpg)

## Come by and putt with us!

We warmly invite all disc golf fans to try out the area. Whether you want to work on your technique or simply play a relaxed round – Sportpionierpark offers the perfect setting. And who knows? Maybe we'll see each other soon for a little challenge at the wooden obstacle!
