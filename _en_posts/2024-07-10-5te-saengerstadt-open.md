From 5 to 7 July, we got to play at the [5th Sängerstadt Open – feels like family](https://discgonauts.de/saengerstadt-open-2024-ein-rueckblick/).

## Detours improve your local knowledge

Since we decided to travel by train from Cologne to Finsterwalde, we stocked up on plenty of provisions beforehand.
Unexpectedly, we arrived in Leipzig with only about 2.5 hours of delays and several diversions.
We were tired, but still in good spirits.


## The tournament

The [course in lovely Finsterwalde](https://udisc.com/courses/buergerheide-finsterwalde-9bO6) offers plenty of variety and hosted a total of four rounds (a doubles round on Friday and three tournament rounds).

![Arrived](/assets/images/posts/2407_finsterwalde_arrival_mod.jpg)

Since the event had several sponsors, we were treated to a generous player pack.
In addition to a [midrange from Wingman](https://www.wingmandiscs.com/en/havran), we were supplied with sunscreen, mosquito spray and other goodies.

Fittingly, the tournament was opened by the "singers of Finsterwalde".

The catering by the [Discgonauts](https://discgonauts.de/) was superb.
Since, in addition to the course, they have also restored an old [school garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_garden), there was running water, electricity and a kitchen. Where else do you find that?
The highlight was a cargo bike with ice cream that made the rounds of the tees.

The weather mostly played along, too. Although the storm on Saturday evening was so strong that fallen trees had to be cleared out of the way until late at night, the tournament was able to continue without disruption.
The weather was even so good that the sunscreen in the player pack turned out to be a great idea.

In the end, Ben and Raphael both finished 5th in their divisions ([results on Metrix](https://discgolfmetrix.com/2825113)) and were happy to receive prizes from the sponsor [discgolf4you](https://discgolf4you.com).
