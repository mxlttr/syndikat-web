On Saturday, 17 October, Disc Golf Syndikat celebrated the end of the summer season at Scheibenstraße. 19 players took part and played two rounds. These gave the day a sporting framework, but it was shaped just as much by the encounters and conversations at the course. The league summer thus came to a close with the community at its centre.

## A new layout to end the season

The autumn festival used its own 13-hole layout, including four new holes. This gave the familiar course a different flow. Even regulars got to experience Scheibenstraße in a new way and had to adapt to unfamiliar lines and challenges.

![Participants on their way to the next hole](/assets/images/posts/herbstfest-2026-unterwegs.jpg)

## Honouring the summer winners

The awards focused on the winners of the summer season of our [Syndication Weekly](https://udisc.com/leagues/syndication-weekly-offene-liga-koeln-dkb2vB/standings?seasonId=season_iepthr6ch9ojdfly7avrpztn&division=GEN). Looking back at the achievements of the past months gave the league season a festive finish. A big thank you to [Thrownature](https://thrownatur-discgolf.de/), whose support made the prizes for the award ceremony possible. Thanks also to the sports office of the Nippes district for letting us use the course, and to the groundskeeper, who prepared everything superbly.

![Three participants at the summer winners' award ceremony](/assets/images/posts/herbstfest-2026-ehrung.jpg)

A total of 42 different players took part across the 29 Tuesday dates. On average, 12.8 players turned up per evening. The record of 21 participants was reached twice: on 21 April and on 1 September.

## Around the autumn festival

Moritz went the extra mile as the organiser of the autumn festival. Thank you, Moritz – and thanks to everyone who helped out!

The participants contributed various dishes to the buffet. It was ready after the rounds and provided a welcome refuel at the course.

## Autumn weather with consequences

The day showed its autumnal side: it was cool, but there were also spells of bright sunshine. Right on cue for the start of the second round, heavy rain set in. The wet conditions made playing more demanding and shook up the leaderboard once more.

![Participants with umbrellas over their disc golf carts on the wet course](/assets/images/posts/herbstfest-2026-regen.jpg)

## Results from two rounds

In MPO, Nils Teckenberg won with −5. Erik Steup and Elmar Bode shared second place with −3 each. The FPO division was won by Sarah Ziegler with +1. Full scores and standings are available on UDisc for [round 1](https://udisc.com/events/herbstfest-26-HcmQu5/leaderboard?round=1) and [round 2](https://udisc.com/events/herbstfest-26-HcmQu5/leaderboard?round=2).

![FPO award ceremony at the autumn festival](/assets/images/posts/herbstfest-2026-fpo-ehrung.jpg)

Once again, the autumn festival showed how much joy the course at Scheibenstraße brings to our community. We are looking forward to the next rounds!
