Originally, I had planned to travel to Schmallenberg even without a spot in the field, to take photos at the tournament and enjoy the breathtaking atmosphere of the golf course. Philipp and I had rented a holiday flat for the weekend together with Severin from the [Bonnsai](https://bonnsai.org/). However, since only the Bonn player had a guaranteed spot thanks to his rating, we planned to at least use Thursday to play a round on the practice day.

Surprisingly, I did get a spot in early April after working my way up to first place on the waiting list. My rating was just enough to take part in this prestigious tournament. Philipp offered to be my caddy, and so nothing stood in the way of my third appearance at the Big Arm Open.

![Max jump putting at the Big Arm Open 3](/assets/images/posts/big_arm_jump_putt.jpg)

On Thursday morning, we set off for the idyllic Sauerland in a fully loaded Smart – packed with disc golf discs as well as rain gear and summer clothes, since the weather was forecast to be changeable. Arriving at Golfclub Schmallenberg, we teamed up at the first tee with Björn and Lukas, who had also only come for the practice round. The sun was shining and conditions were perfect. I shot a +1, setting the bar for the first tournament round.

A round on the course takes about 4 hours and covers 7 kilometres through a picturesque landscape with plenty of elevation. The challenge begins right at hole 1, a 325-metre par 5 that, with its 40 metres of elevation change, demands both technique and power.

![View of hole 1 at the Big Arm Open 3 in Schmallenberg](/assets/images/posts/big_arm_bahn_1.jpg)


## Friday, round 1 — the weather turns

The next day, I started the first round at midday. True to form, I botched my drive on the iconic hole 1, and together with Marian and Patrick from Cologne and Manuel from Recklinghausen, we fought our way through the 18 holes. Nobody in the flight was happy with their performance; the practice round had raised high expectations that we unfortunately couldn't meet. This time I only managed a +12, because I threw my discs out of bounds too often.

Feeling down, I found out in the clubhouse that many other players had fared the same. The weather, the wind and the tournament pressure meant that many couldn't repeat the previous day's scores.

Severin, who hadn't been able to play a practice round but knew the course from the previous year, shot an impressive –5 with seven birdies. Christian Plaue, playing in the last flight of the day, brought home the hot round with 15 birdies and three pars. Incredible!

## Saturday, round 2 — back to square one

After the disappointing first day, I teed off for the second round at 9 am, determined not to let the previous results affect me. Conditions were ideal, and I was optimistic that things would go better this time.

My first throw on hole 1, however, was another false start, and I "shanked" my driver to the right. But things improved: with two birdies, five bogeys and only two out-of-bounds throws, I was more than happy with a +3 in the end.

I got to know three friendly disc golfers and enjoyed a wonderful day on the course. After my round, I caddied for Severin, who wanted to put pressure on the lead card from the second-to-last flight of the day. Unfortunately, he "only" managed 5 over par.

![Max at the Big Arm Open 3 in Schmallenberg](/assets/images/posts/big-arm-max.jpg)

## Sunday, round 3 — the final

On the final day of the Big Arm Open 3, I had a similar experience to before. The wind was strong and blew my putter out of the chains on hole 7 – normally you would expect a disc from four metres to stay in the basket.

In the end, I played an unspectacular +5 and finished 54th in the Open division. [All results can be found on turniere.discgolf.de](https://turniere.discgolf.de/index.php?p=events&sp=live&id=2006). Kerstin and Raphael had travelled especially to follow my round and cheer me on.

![Putting on the golf club course at the Big Arm Open 3](/assets/images/posts/big_arm_putt.jpg)

## Conclusion — an unforgettable tournament

A big thank you goes to TV Fredeburg and Golfclub Schmallenberg for the professional hosting and organisation of the tournament. Special thanks to the tournament directors Sven and Dirk, who put together a spectacular event for the third time. The Big Arm Open 3 was not only a showcase of crazy drives, but also a valuable opportunity to experience the community of disc golf. Long putts were celebrated, and after small mishaps, the rest of the flight lifted their opponents back up and cheered them on.

Next year, the 2025 German Championship takes place on the same course. Anyone who has never been there shouldn't miss the opportunity and should think about making a day trip to the Sauerland to watch.
