On 26 October, around 25 disc golfers met in the autumn sunshine on our course at Scheibenstraße in Cologne to celebrate the season finale together at Disc Golf Syndikat's autumn festival. With participants from our own club and neighbouring clubs, the season was rounded off in a relaxed atmosphere with some sporting challenges.

### Team spirit and precision: doubles and singles

The sporting programme started with a doubles round, in which players competed in teams of two for the best score. In the end, Matthias and Fred took first place with a strong –7 over 9 holes and celebrated their success as a team. The casual round created a relaxed mood and was a great start to the day.

![Action from the doubles competition at the autumn festival](/assets/images/posts/converted-3.jpg)

This was followed by the singles round, in which everyone competed individually. Here, Max, a member of Disc Golf Syndikat, came out on top with a score of –4. Overall, the results were close, and the round was a lovely sporting finale for all players.

![Winners' photo of the singles round at the autumn festival](/assets/images/posts/herbstfest-gewinner.jpg)

### CTP challenge: a special precision task

Another highlight of the day was the "Closest to the Pin" (CTP) challenge. The aim was to throw the disc as close as possible to the basket on hole 7 – an exercise that requires concentration and precision and offered everyone an exciting change from a classic round.

![Participants in the CTP challenge at the autumn festival](/assets/images/posts/converted-8.jpg)

### Culinary variety: a bring-your-own buffet

Food was taken care of as well – by the guests themselves, who brought a wide variety of delicious dishes and snacks. The spread ranged from salads and pizza rolls to Guido's now legendary date dip, which becomes a favourite all over again at every club party. The variety at the buffet added to the relaxed atmosphere and made the day a culinary experience, too.

![Buffet with dishes brought by guests at the autumn festival](/assets/images/posts/converted-9.jpg)

### Positive feedback and a big thank you

Special thanks go to Nils and Moritz, who planned and organised the entire autumn festival. Their preparation ensured that the event ran smoothly and that everyone had a good time. The positive feedback from our guests, who particularly praised the friendly atmosphere, shows how much the autumn festival was appreciated as a relaxed end to the season.

![Group photo at Disc Golf Syndikat's autumn festival](/assets/images/posts/converted.jpg)

Disc Golf Syndikat is looking forward to many more great rounds and events together in the coming winter season – [the weekly league](/en/events/) now takes place on Sundays at 1 pm.

See you on the course!
