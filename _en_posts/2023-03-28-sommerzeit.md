> **Update:** From 18 April, the league starts at 7 pm!

The days are getting longer again! We are happy to let you know that our weekly league now takes place **every Tuesday at 6:30 pm** again! [You can find the event on UDisc](https://udisc.com/leagues/syndication-weekly); there is no need to register in advance.

The league takes place on the beautiful [disc golf course at Scheibenstraße](https://goo.gl/maps/yqyVDEoEs8Qd5LD56) and gives players of all experience levels the chance to improve their game and have a good time. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced player, you are very welcome to join our weekly league rounds.

![League layout](/assets/images/posts/scheibenstrasse_layout.jpg)

**From 18 April**, we will move the league to **7:00 pm** because of the changing daylight. We want to make sure everyone has the chance to play a relaxed round after work.

When winter time begins, we will switch back to Sunday for our regular league meet-up.

We look forward to welcoming you at our weekly league and hope you will join us to improve your disc golf skills and make new friends.

If you have any questions or suggestions, you can reach us [by email](mailto:birdie@syndikat.golf) or on our <a href="#" onclick="window.open('\/\/community.syndikat.golf');">Discord server</a>.

With that in mind – see you on the course.
