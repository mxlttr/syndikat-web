In the summer of 2021, the wish for another disc golf club in the Cologne area grew. The small course at Scheibenstraße was filling up more and more with new players who had found their way to the disc during the coronavirus pandemic.

After the usual wrangling with various authorities and German bureaucracy, the time had finally come in January 2022:

A name had been found, the club was registered and the first members started trickling in.

We have since grown into a group of disc golf enthusiasts who meet regularly to improve their skills and enjoy the game.

Everyone is welcome with us, regardless of your skill or experience. We offer a friendly and welcoming environment where you can feel comfortable and at home. Our community is open and inclusive, and we are always happy to welcome new members.

As a club, we regularly organise training sessions, tournaments and events for our members and for anyone interested in disc golf.

We believe that disc golf not only promotes physical fitness but also enriches the mind and soul. So if you are looking for a new challenge and want to become part of a great community, feel free to visit us at one of our training sessions or events in Cologne-Weidenpesch. We would love to meet you and show you how much fun disc golf can be.

We hope to welcome you soon!
