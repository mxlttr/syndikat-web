## Syndication Weekly

Our weekly league. Players of every level are welcome. One scored round, followed by free play until the sun goes down.

> Every Tuesday, 6:30 pm, [at Scheibenstraße](https://goo.gl/maps/yqyVDEoEs8Qd5LD56) – [event on UDisc.com](https://udisc.com/leagues/syndication-weekly)

If you are looking for a broader introduction, [Disc Golf in Cologne](/en/disc-golf-koeln/) also gives you an overview of the course, training and club.
