Do you want to play tournaments, connect with like-minded people and help make disc golf better known in Cologne and Germany?

Then we look forward to your registration!

If you would first like to understand how getting started works via the course, league and training in Cologne, take a look at [Disc Golf in Cologne](/en/disc-golf-koeln/).

Choose your billing period here. Your membership renews automatically unless you cancel it. We use [Stripe](https://stripe.com/en-de) as our payment provider. No data is stored on our servers. If you have questions about membership or payment options, [write to us via the contact form](/en/contact/).

{% include components/plan_selection.html %}

To change or cancel your membership or to update your payment details, you can [manage your account in the Stripe dashboard](https://billing.stripe.com/p/login/3cseY17778iO6Vq6oo).

### Benefits

- DFV number (required for official tournaments)
- [Club training](/en/training/)
- Insurance at tournaments
- 15% off at [frisbeeshop.com](https://frisbeeshop.com)
- Member access on the Discord server

### Donations

If you would like to make a donation, [you will find a payment form here](https://buy.stripe.com/5kAdULfSzh02eOIdQV) that lets you send us any amount.

If you have any questions, [write to us via the contact form](/en/contact/).
