At our club training, players of all levels meet to practise together, learn new techniques and share the fun of disc golf. Our weekly training takes place every Friday at 4:00 pm on the [course at Scheibenstraße](https://goo.gl/maps/yqyVDEoEs8Qd5LD56). Under the guidance of our experienced coaches, we work on throwing accuracy, technique and tactics, so that beginners and advanced players alike get their money's worth.

If you are new to the sport or new to the city, our page [Disc Golf in Cologne](/en/disc-golf-koeln/) gives you the quickest overview of the course, league, training and club.

To take part, please sign up using the form below so that we can prepare the training as well as possible. We look forward to seeing you and to many exciting rounds on the course!
