Protecting your personal data is important to us. Below, in accordance with the applicable data protection laws, in particular the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we inform you about the processing of your data when you use our website syndikat.golf.

## 1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the GDPR for the processing of your personal data on this website is:

Disc Golf Syndikat e.V., Köln<br>Email: [birdie@syndikat.golf](mailto:birdie@syndikat.golf)

## 2. Types of data processed

We process personal data that you voluntarily provide to us when using our website. This includes in particular:

- Contact details (e.g. name, email address, phone number) that you provide to us via contact forms or email.
- Communication data (e.g. IP address, date and time of the server request) that is collected automatically when you visit our website.
- Payment data collected in connection with a donation or other transactions via our website, where applicable.

## 3. Purposes of data processing

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

- To answer enquiries and communicate with you.
- To carry out transactions if you make donations or other payments via our website.
- To improve and ensure the functionality of our website.
- To comply with legal obligations, in particular with regard to tax and accounting requirements.

## 4. Disclosure of data

We only pass on your data to third parties if this is permitted by law or if you have given your consent. Your data is transferred to third parties in particular if this is necessary for the performance of a contract or if we are required to do so by a legal obligation.

## 5. Use of cookies

Our website uses cookies to make using the website easier and to improve the online experience. You can deactivate the use of cookies in your browser settings, but this may impair the functionality of our website.

## 6. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Nevertheless, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection with regard to your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact details given above.

## 8. Changes to this privacy policy

We reserve the right to change this privacy policy at any time. We publish the current version on our website. The version published at any given time applies.

## 9. Contact

If you have any questions or comments about this privacy policy, you can reach us using the contact details given above.

Last updated: 18 April 2024

Thank you for your trust and attention.
