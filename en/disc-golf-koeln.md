If you want to play disc golf in Cologne, sooner or later you will end up with the same questions: Where can you actually throw? How do you connect with others? And is this something for complete beginners, or only for people with a full bag and tournament experience? The short answer: getting started in Cologne is pretty straightforward once you know where to begin.

For many people, the first sensible route leads to Scheibenstraße in Weidenpesch. A large part of the local community meets there, and that is also where our weekly league takes place. If you are new, you don't have to be a member first or be able to throw perfectly. The best way in is usually to come to the Weekly once and join our Discord server at the same time.

> The easiest way in: <a href="/en/events/" data-umami-event="koeln_weekly_click">take part in the Weekly</a> and join the <a href="#" data-umami-event="koeln_discord_click" onclick="window.open('\/\/community.syndikat.golf');">community on Discord</a>.

## Where can you play disc golf in Cologne?

The most important place for many players in Cologne is the course at Scheibenstraße in Weidenpesch. Not only do casual rounds take place there, but also many of Disc Golf Syndikat's regular club activities. If you don't just want to throw a few discs on your own but also want to meet other players, it is usually the best place to start.

Our <a href="/en/events/" data-umami-event="koeln_weekly_click">events</a> are the most direct way into the weekly league. That is exactly where you should start if you want to get to know people in Cologne quickly and play your first round.

Besides the course, Cologne now also has the [putting area in Sportpionierpark](/en/blog/sportpionierpark/). It is not a full round like at Scheibenstraße, but an exciting addition for short sessions, technique work and relaxed putting.

## Who is disc golf in Cologne suitable for?

Disc golf in Cologne is not a sport just for tournament players. Beginners, casual players and experienced throwers regularly come together on the same course. That is exactly what makes getting started easier: you don't have to train alone for months before you are allowed to show up anywhere.

If you are new to the sport, a few basics and very modest equipment are enough to start with. If you are just starting out, our [guide for disc golf beginners](/en/blog/leitfaden-fuer-einsteiger/) and the post on [beginner discs](/en/blog/die-besten-scheiben-fuer-discgolf-einsteiger/) will also help you.

If you have been playing for a while, you will still find plenty of connections in Cologne: group rounds, the league, club life and a community that makes more possible than just the occasional after-work round.

## Why is the Weekly the best way in?

Our weekly league is called **Syndication Weekly**. According to the <a href="/en/events/" data-umami-event="koeln_weekly_click">events page</a>, we currently meet on Tuesdays at 6:30 pm at Scheibenstraße. Players of every level are welcome. We play one scored round, and afterwards people often keep playing freely.

Especially for people in Cologne looking to connect quickly, the Weekly is the best way in. You see right away how others play, get to know the course and find it easier to get talking with the local scene. If you only want to watch, walk along or join a relaxed round at first, that is completely fine too.

Above all: beginners are welcome. You don't have to show up with big ambitions. In many cases, it is enough to simply be there, get to know a few people and play your first round.

## Why is the Discord worth it?

The Discord server is the easiest digital way into the Cologne disc golf community around the Syndikat. There you find out more quickly what is coming up, can ask questions and more easily find people for rounds, events or spontaneous meet-ups.

If you are unsure whether you can just turn up at the Weekly, Discord is often the best first step. You get a quick response there and don't have to piece everything together from scattered pages.

## How do I become part of the disc golf community in Cologne?

If you first want to understand how the Syndikat is organised, take a look at our [About us page](/en/about/). There you will find the club's background and an overview of how the community in Cologne has developed.

If you want to get straight onto the course with people, the <a href="/en/events/" data-umami-event="koeln_weekly_click">events</a> and the <a href="#" data-umami-event="koeln_discord_click" onclick="window.open('\/\/community.syndikat.golf');">Discord server</a> are the most practical starting points. If you want to stay for the long term, play tournaments or help support the club, you will find the next steps towards membership under [Become a member](/en/register/).

Above all: you don't have to know everything right away. The quickest way in is to show up once, throw a few discs and get talking to people who are already regulars at Scheibenstraße.

## Conclusion: how to get started with disc golf in Cologne

If you want to start playing disc golf in Cologne, or play more regularly again, the most obvious way in is clear: drop by the Weekly at Scheibenstraße and join the community on Discord at the same time.

You don't have to be perfectly prepared for the first step. You just need to know where you can turn up. That is exactly what our <a href="/en/events/" data-umami-event="koeln_weekly_click">events</a>, the [club page](/en/about/), the <a href="#" data-umami-event="koeln_discord_click" onclick="window.open('\/\/community.syndikat.golf');">Discord server</a> and [membership](/en/register/) are for.
