_This is an English translation for your convenience. Only the [German version](/satzung/) is legally binding._

# § 1 Name, registered office and financial year

1. The name of the club is: "Disc Golf Syndikat"
1. Its registered office is in Cologne and it is to be entered in the register of associations at the Cologne Local Court (Amtsgericht Köln). After registration, it adds the suffix e.V. to its name. The club's address is determined by the board.
1. The club's financial year is the calendar year.

# § 2 Purpose of the club and non-profit status

1. The purpose of the club is the cultivation, promotion and spread of the sport of "disc golf" (§ 52 (2) no. 21 of the German Fiscal Code) in Cologne and the surrounding region.
The club focuses on community and togetherness, in particular by giving disc golf beginners the opportunity to get to know the sport and its "spirit".
In addition, the club enables its members to take part in tournaments of the state association and the German Frisbee Sport Association (DFV).
1. The purpose of the statutes is realised in particular by:
  - holding regular training sessions
  - being willing to help other disc golf players
  - holding sport-specific club events
  - taking part in tournaments and sporting competitions
1. The club shall become a member of Deutscher Frisbeesport-Verband e.V. and Frisbeesport-Landesverband Nordrhein-Westfalen e.V. The club pursues exclusively and directly non-profit purposes within the meaning of the section "Tax-privileged purposes" of the German Fiscal Code.
The club acts selflessly; it does not primarily pursue its own economic interests. The club's funds may only be used for purposes in accordance with the statutes. Members do not receive any payments from the club's funds. No person may benefit from expenses that are unrelated to the purpose of the club or from disproportionately high remuneration.

# § 3 Acquisition of membership, honorary membership

1. Any natural person can become a member of the club. Admission is granted by the board after receipt of a written application for membership. For minors, the consent of their legal representative is required.
1. The executive board decides on admission by resolution with a simple majority. Membership begins with the resolution. By submitting the signed application for membership, the member acknowledges the club's statutes and regulations in their currently valid version.
1. There is no entitlement to admission. A rejection of admission does not need to be justified. There is no legal remedy against the rejection of admission.
1. Any natural person, including posthumously, who has rendered outstanding services to the club or its aims can be proposed as an honorary member by the board and confirmed by the general meeting.

# § 4 Termination of membership

1. Membership ends through resignation, death or exclusion from the club.
1. The declaration of resignation must be sent in writing to the club's address. Resignation is possible with six weeks' notice to the end of the year.
1. A member can be excluded by the board if they
  - have failed to meet their membership fee obligations for more than 12 months despite a reminder,
  - are guilty of conduct damaging to the club. In this case, the board is obliged to hear the member before the exclusion.

# § 5 Admission fees and membership fees

1. Members are obliged to pay membership fees. In addition, admission fees, levies and fees for special services of the club may be charged.
1. The amount of the admission fees and membership fees is determined by the general meeting.
1. Members are obliged to inform the club of any changes to their bank details, address and email address.
1. Honorary members are exempt from membership fees.

# § 6 Voting rights and eligibility, votes

1. All club members who are not in arrears with their membership fees and have reached the age of 12 are entitled to vote.
1. Voting rights can only be exercised in person.
1. All members of the club who are of legal age and have full legal capacity are eligible for election to the board and as auditors. Unless otherwise specified, votes are decided by a simple majority. In the event of a tie, a motion is deemed rejected.

# § 7 Bodies of the club

The bodies of the club are:

1. the general meeting
1. the board

# § 8 General&shy;meeting

1. The general meeting is the highest body of the club. It takes place once a year in the second half of the financial year.
1. The board must send the invitation to the general meeting, stating the agenda, to the members by email 14 days before the date.
1. The agenda must contain the following items:
  - Approval of the minutes of the last general meeting
  - Report of the board and financial report
  - Report of the auditors
  - Discharge of the board
  - Elections
  - Resolution on the budget
  - Resolution on submitted motions
1. Motions can be submitted by the club's bodies and by any member. Motions to amend the statutes must be listed separately in the agenda.
1. Amendments to the statutes can only be adopted with a two-thirds majority of the members present who are entitled to vote.
1. Motions that are not listed in the agenda can only be dealt with if the urgency of dealing with them is confirmed by a two-thirds majority.
1. The board must convene an extraordinary general meeting with 14 days' notice if the board has decided to do so or if twenty percent of the members have requested it in writing.
1. Every duly convened general meeting has a quorum.
1. Minutes of the general meeting must be taken and signed by the chair or their deputy and by the secretary or a minute-taker elected by the meeting.

# § 9 Board

1. The board is elected by the general meeting for two years. It manages the club's affairs. It consists of:
  1. the 1st Chair,
  1. the 2nd Chair,
  1. the Treasurer,
  1. the Board Member for Public Relations,
  1. and the Board Member for Equality.
1. The board within the meaning of § 26 BGB (German Civil Code) consists of the 1st and 2nd Chair.
1. The task of the executive board is to lead and manage the club. It is responsible for all tasks that are not assigned to another club body by the statutes or regulations.
1. If a board member leaves office during the financial year, the board is entitled to fill this office on an interim basis until the next general meeting.
1. The board can pass resolutions by a simple majority.

# § 10 Committees

1. The board can set up committees for special tasks if required.

# § 11 Audit

The club's financial affairs must be audited at least once a year after the end of the financial year by two auditors. They are elected by the general meeting for two years. They must present an audit report to the general meeting.

# § 12 Dissolution of the club

1. The dissolution of the club can only be decided at a general meeting convened expressly and exclusively for this purpose.
1. The meeting has a quorum if at least 50% of the members entitled to vote are present. If attendance is lower, a new meeting must be convened, which has a quorum in any case. This must be pointed out in the invitation.
1. The dissolution of the club can only be decided with a majority of 75% of the members present who are entitled to vote.
1. In the event of the dissolution of the club or the discontinuation of its previous purpose, the club's assets shall pass to "NABU – Naturschutzbund Deutschland e.V.", Charitéstraße 3, 10117 Berlin, which must use them directly and exclusively for non-profit purposes.

# § 13 Validity of these statutes

1. These statutes were adopted by the general meeting on 8 June 2021.
1. These statutes come into force upon entry in the register of associations.
1. All previous statutes cease to be valid at that time.
