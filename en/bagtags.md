## Rules:

* Every player must carry their bag tag visibly on their bag.
* Forgetting your bag tag is penalised with a €2 donation to the club funds.
* The better number decides in which round the challenge is accepted.
* If bag tag holders play in the same flight at a tournament or at the Weekly, the tags are automatically at stake!
* Challenges across flights only by prior agreement! In the event of a tie, the better number wins!
* Challenged players have 4 weeks to find a date to play for the bag tags.

## Forgot your bag tag?

If the penalty fee cannot be paid in cash, [it can be paid here via Stripe](https://donate.stripe.com/9AQ9Evayf6lo4a48wC).
